When downloading the linux installer, the install.sh and remove.sh scripts are not executable.
The permissiona are the same for the entire bundle:

    For files: 644
    For directories: 755

---

Workaround:\
Open bundle in cmd and type:
```console
chmod +x install.sh
chmod +x remove.sh
```
